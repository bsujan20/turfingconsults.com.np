Hydropower Scheme

Typical run-of-river layout.

Water is diverted at the intake, cleaned of gravel and sediment, carried along the headrace at a gentle gradient, then dropped through the penstock to spin the turbines in the powerhouse.

Swipe to explore the scheme →

  1. 1

    Intake

    Diverts part of the river flow through a trash rack into the waterway.

  2. 2

    Gravel Trap

    Settles out gravel and coarse bed load just after the intake.

  3. 3

    Desander

    Slows the flow so fine sediment settles before it can wear the turbines.

  4. 4

    Headrace

    Carries water at a gentle gradient along the hillside, gaining head over the river.

  5. 5

    Surge / Forebay

    Steadies the flow and absorbs pressure surges ahead of the penstock.

  6. 6

    Penstock

    Drops the water steeply under pressure, turning head into energy.

  7. 7

    Powerhouse

    Turbines and generators produce electricity, then water returns to the river.

Message from the Chairman

At Turfing Technical Consults, our work begins where the terrain gets difficult — steep valleys, seasonal rivers, and remote sites across Nepal's hills and mountains. Every hydropower and civil engineering project we undertake is built on rigorous field investigation, sound engineering judgment, and hands-on supervision through to commissioning.

As Chairman, my commitment is simple: to deliver technically sound, buildable engineering solutions that stand up to Nepal's demanding terrain and genuinely serve the communities and clients who depend on them. We look forward to partnering with you on your next project.

Our Approach

From investigation
to commissioning.

Every scheme moves through the same five stages, in the same order.

  1. Investigation & Survey

    Site walkover, topographic survey, and river gauging to establish ground truth.

  2. Hydrology & Hydraulics

    Flow analysis and hydraulic modeling to size the scheme correctly.

  3. Feasibility & Planning

    Layout options assessed for cost, capacity, and constructability.

  4. Detailed Engineering

    Full structural and hydraulic design, ready for construction tender.

  5. Construction Support

    Site supervision through to commissioning of the powerhouse.
